About Loxley

Construction knowledge. Roofing expertise. The Loxley standard.

Loxley Roofing and Construction approaches every property as a complete system. From the structural deck and waterproofing layers to ventilation, flashing and final inspection, our work is built around careful planning, clear communication and lasting protection.

We serve residential and commercial property owners throughout the St. Louis area with detailed inspections, professional documentation and construction solutions designed around the needs of each property.
