Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026

Loxley Roofing and Construction ("Loxley," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us. When you submit our inspection request form, we collect the details you enter — your name, phone number, property address, a description of what you need, and, if you provide them, your email and service preferences.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your inspection request or project.
  • To prepare an estimate and schedule work.
  • To respond to questions you send us.

We use your information only to serve you. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing.

How your request is delivered

Form submissions are delivered to Loxley by email (or, where configured, through a form-processing service that transmits your message to us). These providers process your submission solely to deliver it to us.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. If we add website analytics in the future to understand general, aggregated traffic, we will update this policy and honor your browser's privacy preferences.

Data retention

We keep inspection requests and project records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal, warranty, and business requirements, then dispose of them securely.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the information you have submitted. Contact us using the details below and we will respond promptly.
